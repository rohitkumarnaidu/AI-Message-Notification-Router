# Personalization & User Isolation Defense

Personalization Axes:
1. Receiving-User Isolation: Evidence retrieval filters strictly on user_id == receiving_user_id.
2. Direct vs Group Context: Direct messages have higher priority than group broadcasts.
3. Group Mute & Direct Mentions: Direct mentions with operational deadlines override group mute settings.
4. Business Relationship & Opt-In/Opt-Out: Opted-in promos digest; unsolicited promos mute as spam.
5. Sender Trust Hierarchy: Personal trust elevates priority but NEVER overrides credential or payment scams.
6. Interaction Ratios: Historical reply/dismiss/report ratios dynamically adjust relevance scoring.
